# Attendance Policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. This policy is fictional and was written for the CampusGPT student
> project. It does not describe any real university.

Category: academic
Effective from: Odd Term 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets the minimum attendance a student must maintain in every course, how
attendance is calculated, and what happens when a student falls short.

1.2 It applies to all undergraduate students in every programme (B.Tech and BBA), in every
course that has scheduled lectures, tutorials or laboratory sessions.

## 2. Minimum attendance requirement

2.1 A student must attend at least **75%** of the classes held in each course during a term.
The requirement applies to each course separately; a high attendance in one course does not
make up for a shortage in another.

2.2 Attendance is calculated as classes attended divided by classes held, from the first day
of the term up to the date of calculation. Lectures, tutorials and laboratory sessions of a
course are counted together.

2.3 Percentages are not rounded up. A student with 74.9% attendance is below the requirement.

2.4 Classes cancelled by the department, and classes on declared holidays, are not counted as
held.

## 3. Consequences of a shortage

3.1 A student whose attendance in a course is below 75% on the last teaching day of the term
is **debarred from the end-term examination of that course**. The course is recorded with the
grade "DB" (debarred) and must be repeated in a later term.

3.2 Being debarred in one course does not affect the student's right to sit the end-term
examinations of the other courses in which the requirement is met.

3.3 Mid-term examinations, quizzes and assignments are not affected by attendance; a
student with a shortage may still take them.

3.4 Departments publish attendance shortage warnings during the term. A student who receives
a warning must meet their faculty mentor within the time stated in the notice and agree a
recovery plan.

## 4. Condonation for medical and approved reasons

4.1 The Dean of Academics may condone a shortage of up to **10 percentage points** (that is,
down to a minimum of **65%**) in a course for one of the following reasons:

- illness or hospitalisation, supported by a certificate from a registered medical
  practitioner;
- representing the university in a sports, cultural or technical event, with a letter from
  the faculty coordinator;
- the death or serious illness of an immediate family member.

4.2 An application for condonation must be submitted to the department office **within seven
days** of the student returning to classes, with the supporting documents. Applications
submitted later are not considered.

4.3 Condonation is not granted below 65% for any reason. A student below 65% in a course is
debarred from that course's end-term examination.

4.4 Condonation can be granted for at most two courses in one term.

## 5. Recording and corrections

5.1 Faculty record attendance for every class on the attendance system within 24 hours of the
class. Students can see their own attendance on the student portal and in CampusGPT.

5.2 A student who believes their attendance has been recorded incorrectly must report it to
the course faculty **within seven days** of the class. Corrections after that period are
made only with the approval of the Head of Department.

5.3 Late arrival of more than 10 minutes may be recorded as absent at the discretion of the
faculty.

## 6. Recovering from a shortage

6.1 There are no extra classes to make up attendance. A student below 75% can recover only by
attending the remaining classes of the term.

6.2 The number of consecutive classes a student must attend to reach 75% is
ceil((0.75 x held - attended) / 0.25). For example, a student who has attended 41 of 60
classes (68.3%) must attend the next 16 classes in a row to reach 57 of 76 (75.0%).

6.3 Students are advised to check their attendance every week and to speak to their faculty
mentor as soon as any course falls below 80%.
