# Scholarship Scheme

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. This scheme is fictional and was written for the CampusGPT student
> project. It does not describe any real university.

Category: scholarships
Effective from: Odd Term 2026

## 1. Scholarships offered

1.1 The university offers three scholarships to undergraduate students:

| Scholarship | Benefit | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Merit scholarship | 100% tuition fee waiver | Top 2% of each programme by CGPA |
| Merit-cum-means scholarship | 50% tuition fee waiver | CGPA and family income |
| Sports excellence scholarship | 25% tuition fee waiver | State or national level representation |

1.2 A student may hold only one scholarship at a time. If eligible for more than one, the
student receives the one with the larger benefit.

1.3 Scholarships cover the tuition fee only. They do not cover examination, hostel or mess
fees.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 **Merit scholarship:** awarded automatically each academic year to the top 2% of students
in each programme and semester, ranked by CGPA, with no backlog (failed or debarred course).

2.2 **Merit-cum-means scholarship:** open to students with a CGPA of at least **8.0**, no
backlog, and an annual family income of not more than **Rs 8,00,000**, supported by an income
certificate issued by the competent authority.

2.3 **Sports excellence scholarship:** open to students who represented their state or the
country in a recognised sport in the previous twelve months, with a CGPA of at least 6.0.

2.4 First-semester students are considered on the basis of their Class XII marks: at least
90% for the merit scholarship and at least 85% for the merit-cum-means scholarship.

## 3. How to apply

3.1 Applications for the merit-cum-means and sports scholarships open once a year; the dates
are announced in a notice. Applications are submitted on the student portal, and the
original documents are verified at the scholarship office.

3.2 Documents needed: the latest grade card, the income certificate (merit-cum-means), the
participation certificates (sports), and a copy of the student's bank passbook.

3.3 Incomplete applications, or applications submitted after the closing date, are not
considered.

## 4. Continuation

4.1 A scholarship is reviewed every term. It continues only if the student:

- keeps a CGPA of at least the level required at the time of award;
- has no backlog;
- has at least **75% attendance in every course**;
- has no finding of academic misconduct.

4.2 A student who loses a scholarship pays the full tuition fee from the next term. The
scholarship can be applied for again in a later year if the student becomes eligible.

## 5. Disbursement

5.1 Scholarships are applied as a reduction of the tuition invoice before it is issued. A
full waiver appears on the invoice as "waived".

5.2 If the tuition fee was already paid when the scholarship is awarded, the amount is
refunded to the student's bank account within 45 days.
