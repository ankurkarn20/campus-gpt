# Academic Integrity Policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. This policy is fictional and was written for the CampusGPT student
> project. It does not describe any real university.

Category: academic
Effective from: Odd Term 2026

## 1. Principles

1.1 Students are expected to submit only their own work, to acknowledge every source they
use, and to follow the rules of every examination and assessment.

## 2. Assignments and late submission

2.1 Assignments are submitted on the learning portal before the deadline stated in the course
plan.

2.2 Late submissions are accepted for up to **48 hours** after the deadline, with a penalty
of **10% of the maximum marks for each 24 hours** (or part of 24 hours) of delay. Work
submitted more than 48 hours late receives zero marks.

2.3 Extensions are granted by the course faculty only for documented medical reasons or
approved university duty, and must be requested before the deadline where possible.

## 3. Misconduct

3.1 The following are academic misconduct:

- **plagiarism**: presenting another person's words, code or ideas as one's own, including
  text produced by an AI tool without acknowledgement where the course plan does not allow it;
- **collusion**: working with others on an assessment meant to be individual;
- **unfair means in examinations**: copying, communicating with others, using unauthorised
  material, or possessing a mobile phone or smart device in the examination hall;
- **impersonation**: taking an assessment on behalf of another student, or arranging for
  someone else to take it;
- **fabrication**: inventing data, results, attendance or medical certificates.

## 4. Similarity checks

4.1 Written assignments and project reports are checked with similarity-detection software.
A similarity of more than **20%** (excluding references and quoted text) is reviewed by the
course faculty.

4.2 Programming assignments may be compared with each other and with public code.

## 5. Penalties

5.1 Penalties depend on the seriousness of the misconduct and on whether it is a first or
repeated offence:

| Offence | First offence | Repeated offence |
|---|---|---|
| Plagiarism or collusion in an assignment | Zero marks for the assignment | Zero marks in the assignments component of the course |
| Unfair means in a quiz or mid-term | Zero marks in that examination | Grade F in the course |
| Unfair means in an end-term examination | Grade F in the course | Suspension for one term |
| Impersonation or fabrication | Suspension for one term | Expulsion |

5.2 Every penalty is recorded in the student's academic file and makes the student
ineligible for scholarships for the following academic year.

## 6. Procedure and appeal

6.1 Suspected misconduct is reported by the faculty to the Academic Integrity Committee,
which informs the student in writing and gives them at least five working days to respond.

6.2 The student may appeal the Committee's decision to the Dean of Academics within **ten
working days**. The Dean's decision is final.
