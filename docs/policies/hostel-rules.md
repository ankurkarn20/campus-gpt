# Hostel Rules

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. These rules are fictional and were written for the CampusGPT student
> project. They do not describe any real university.

Category: hostel
Effective from: Odd Term 2026

## 1. Allotment

1.1 Hostel rooms are allotted for one academic year. First-year students are allotted rooms
at admission; other students apply on the student portal in May for the following year.

1.2 Priority is given to students whose permanent address is more than 100 km from campus.

1.3 Room changes are allowed once a term, with the approval of the hostel warden, if a room
of the same type is free.

## 2. Charges

2.1 Hostel charges per term are **Rs 45,000** for a double-sharing room and **Rs 60,000** for
a single room, payable before the start of the term. Mess charges are **Rs 24,000** per term
and are compulsory for every hostel resident.

2.2 A refundable security deposit of **Rs 10,000** is collected once at allotment and returned
within 30 days of vacating the room, minus any charges for damage.

## 3. Timings

3.1 Hostel gates close at **10:30 pm** and open at **5:30 am**. Students returning after the
gate closes must sign the late-entry register at the security desk.

3.2 Three late entries in one month are reported to the warden, and the student's parents or
guardian are informed.

3.3 During the end-term examination period the hostel library and study rooms stay open
until 1 am.

## 4. Leave and night-out

4.1 A student who will spend a night away from the hostel must apply for leave on the
student portal at least **24 hours** in advance. Leave for a weekend or a holiday may be
applied for together.

4.2 Emergency leave can be approved by the warden on the same day, by phone, and recorded on
the portal within 24 hours of returning.

4.3 Hostel leave does not count as attendance. Classes missed while on hostel leave are
recorded as absent unless condoned under the Attendance Policy.

## 5. Conduct

5.1 Visitors are allowed only in the visitors' lounge, between **4 pm and 7 pm**. Visitors
may not enter residents' rooms.

5.2 Alcohol, tobacco, drugs and cooking appliances (including electric kettles and heaters)
are not permitted in the hostel. Violations are reported to the Disciplinary Committee.

5.3 Ragging in any form is a criminal offence and leads to expulsion from the hostel and the
university.

5.4 Residents are responsible for the furniture in their room. Damage is charged to the
residents of the room.

## 6. Vacating the hostel

6.1 Rooms must be vacated within **three days** after the last end-term examination of the
academic year, unless the student is registered for a summer course or internship on campus.

6.2 The room key and the hostel identity card must be returned to the warden's office, which
issues a clearance slip needed for the security deposit refund.
