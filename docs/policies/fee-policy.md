# Fee Payment and Refund Policy

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. This policy is fictional and was written for the CampusGPT student
> project. It does not describe any real university.

Category: fees
Effective from: Odd Term 2026

## 1. Fees charged each term

1.1 Every student is charged, for each term:

- the **tuition fee** of their programme, due on the date announced in the tuition fee
  notice (normally in the second week of the term);
- the **end-term examination fee** of **Rs 3,500**, due on the date announced in the
  examination fee notice.

1.2 Hostel and mess charges are billed separately by the Hostel Office (see the Hostel
Rules).

1.3 All amounts are in Indian rupees. Fees can be paid on the student portal by UPI, net
banking, debit or credit card, or by bank transfer using the details on the invoice.

## 2. Late payment

2.1 A **late fee of Rs 100 per day** is charged on tuition fees paid after the due date, up
to a maximum of **Rs 5,000**.

2.2 If tuition fees remain unpaid **30 days** after the due date, the student's registration
for the term is put on hold. A student whose registration is on hold cannot see their grades
or receive an admit card until the dues and the late fee are paid.

2.3 There is no late fee on the examination fee, but it must be paid before admit cards are
issued; unpaid examination fees mean no admit card (see the Examination Regulations).

2.4 A partial payment reduces the balance but does not stop the late fee on the unpaid
amount.

## 3. Instalments

3.1 A student facing financial difficulty may apply to the Finance Office for the tuition fee
to be paid in **two instalments**: 50% by the due date and 50% within 60 days.

3.2 The application must be made **before** the due date. No late fee is charged on an
approved instalment paid on time.

3.3 The examination fee cannot be paid in instalments.

## 4. Fee waivers

4.1 Students holding a scholarship that covers the full tuition fee have their tuition
invoice marked "waived". The examination fee is never waived.

4.2 Partial scholarships are applied as a reduction of the tuition invoice.

## 5. Refunds

5.1 A student who withdraws from the university receives a refund of the tuition fee for the
current term as follows:

| Withdrawal | Refund of tuition fee |
|---|---|
| Before the term starts | 100% minus Rs 1,000 processing fee |
| Within 15 days of the term start | 80% |
| 16 to 30 days after the term start | 50% |
| More than 30 days after the term start | No refund |

5.2 The examination fee is refunded in full only if the student withdraws before admit
cards are issued.

5.3 Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 30 days of approval.

## 6. Queries

6.1 Questions about invoices, payments or receipts go to the Finance Office. A payment that
does not appear on the portal within three working days should be reported with the
transaction reference.
