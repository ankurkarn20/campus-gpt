# Examination Regulations

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. These regulations are fictional and were written for the CampusGPT
> student project. They do not describe any real university.

Category: examinations
Effective from: Odd Term 2026

## 1. Assessment components

1.1 Every theory course is assessed out of 100 marks:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Quizzes | 15% |
| Assignments | 20% |
| Mid-term examination | 25% |
| End-term examination | 40% |

1.2 Laboratory courses are assessed entirely by continuous evaluation and a final practical
examination, as described in the course plan.

1.3 The minimum pass mark in a course is **40 out of 100** overall, with at least **30%** of
the marks in the end-term examination.

## 2. Eligibility to sit the end-term examination

2.1 A student may sit the end-term examination of a course only if all of the following hold:

- attendance in that course is at least 75%, or has been condoned under the Attendance Policy
  (minimum 65%);
- the end-term examination fee for the term has been paid in full;
- there are no tuition fee dues for the term, unless the Finance Office has approved an
  instalment plan.

2.2 Admit cards are issued on the student portal five days before the first end-term
examination. **Admit cards are not issued to students with unpaid examination fees or unpaid
tuition fees.** A student without an admit card is not allowed into the examination hall.

2.3 A student debarred for attendance in a course receives an admit card for the remaining
courses only.

## 3. Conduct in the examination hall

3.1 Students must be seated **15 minutes** before the start of the examination and must carry
their university identity card and admit card.

3.2 No student is allowed to enter more than **30 minutes** after the start, or to leave
before **one hour** has passed.

3.3 Mobile phones, smart watches and any electronic device other than a permitted
non-programmable calculator must be left outside the hall. Possession of a phone in the hall
is treated as unfair means, whether or not it is used.

3.4 Cases of unfair means are dealt with under the Academic Integrity Policy.

## 4. Missed examinations

4.1 Quizzes and mid-term examinations missed for documented medical reasons may be retaken
once, on a date set by the course faculty. The application, with a medical certificate, must
be made within **five working days** of the missed examination.

4.2 There is no retake for a missed end-term examination. A student who misses an end-term
examination must register for the supplementary examination.

## 5. Supplementary examinations

5.1 A student who fails a course, or misses its end-term examination, may take a
supplementary examination held within six weeks of the declaration of results.

5.2 The supplementary examination replaces the end-term examination only; marks for quizzes,
assignments and the mid-term are carried forward.

5.3 The fee for a supplementary examination is **Rs 1,500 per course**.

5.4 Students debarred for attendance are not eligible for the supplementary examination and
must repeat the course.

## 6. Results, re-evaluation and grades

6.1 Results are declared within four weeks of the last end-term examination.

6.2 A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-term answer script within **seven days**
of the declaration of results, on payment of **Rs 1,000 per course**. Marks after
re-evaluation are final, whether they go up or down.

6.3 Letter grades and grade points:

| Marks | Grade | Grade point |
|---|---|---|
| 90-100 | O | 10 |
| 80-89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70-79 | A | 8 |
| 60-69 | B+ | 7 |
| 50-59 | B | 6 |
| 45-49 | C | 5 |
| 40-44 | P | 4 |
| below 40 | F | 0 |
| debarred | DB | 0 |

6.4 The Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is the credit-weighted average of the grade
points in the term; the CGPA is the same average over all terms completed.
